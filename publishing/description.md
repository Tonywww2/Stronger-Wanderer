# Stronger Wanderer

## English

The Deep Dark has a new reason to fear the Warden. **Stronger Wanderer** gives this formidable creature five powerful combat moves, turning encounters into fierce battles of leaping slams, relentless pursuit and phantom assaults.

- **Lion’s Claw** — A forward somersault followed by a crushing two-arm slam.
- **Follow-up Claw** — A sudden second leap that punishes opponents who think the attack is over.
- **Gravity Leap** — Phantom slams lead into the Warden’s own impact and expanding gravity bursts.
- **Forward Phantom Rush** — A succession of cyan phantoms charges ahead, with the Warden close behind.
- **Side Phantom Rush** — Offset phantoms assault the target from staggered positions before the Warden rushes in.

Stay alert, keep moving, and never assume that a little distance will keep you safe.

## 中文

幽匿深处的威胁，变得更加凶猛。**Stronger Wanderer** 为坚守者带来五种强力招式，以翻身重砸、连续追击与幻影突袭，让每次遭遇都更具压迫感。

- **普通狮子斩**：向前空翻，以双臂重砸落点周围的敌人。
- **追加狮子斩**：在第一次重砸后突然追加跳劈，让松懈的对手措手不及。
- **重力劈跃**：幻影接连跳劈，本体随后重砸，引发逐渐扩大的重力爆发。
- **正向连续幻影突进**：青蓝色分身依次冲锋，本体紧随其后，沿途追击敌人。
- **侧向幻影突进**：错位分身展开交错突袭，随后本体突进压近目标。

保持警惕，不断移动——拉开距离，也未必能够摆脱它的追击。
